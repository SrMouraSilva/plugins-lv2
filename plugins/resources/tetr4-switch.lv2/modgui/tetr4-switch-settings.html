Each card is a preset. Flip a switch up to turn that CV output on while the preset is active; tap the round button to play the preset, and the one beside it to assign the preset to a control of your device, such as a footswitch.

Choose the preset with a single control: a knob, or a footswitch that steps through the presets. The tile shows the preset that is playing.

Screen pop-ups

Show an alert with the preset name on the device screen whenever it changes. Link each one to a page of the device: it only appears on pages that have one.

The MOD Duo has no widgets on its screen, so these pop-ups are not available on it.

MIDI out

Control changes

When the preset changes, send one MIDI control change for each CV output: 127 if the output is on in that preset, 0 if it is off. Connect the MIDI out jack of the plugin to your MIDI gear.
