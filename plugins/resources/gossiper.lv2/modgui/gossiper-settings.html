Footswitches

Link a control that turns on and off, such as a button of your MIDI foot controller, to each row. Gossiper then shows its name when it changes and sends it out as a CV signal (0 V off, 10 V on). Type the name you want the pop-up to say.

Screen pop-ups

A pop-up only appears on a device page that has one of these linked to it. Link each slot to a page where you want to see the alerts (up to four pages). With none linked, Gossiper stays silent.
