Custom pattern

Write your own rhythm. Tap a step to change its level: cut, then half, then pass, and back to cut. A cut lowers the signal by Depth, pass leaves it alone, half is in between. Pattern plays it when it is set to Custom.

Turn the effect on and off with a footswitch. Choose Momentary in the dialog to chop only while you hold it, as with the switch on a guitar.
